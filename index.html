Who we are

Your team on the ground.

An international fashion buying and sourcing partner rooted in South India.

Meet your sourcing partner →

“We bridge the gap between suppliers and global buyers with care, clarity and control.”

JK Fashions Global Sourcing is an apparel sourcing organization based in Tirupur, South India, with operations spanning Tirupur and Chennai. We help suppliers showcase their products to international markets and support buyers in finding the right manufacturing partners.

From product development and costing to supplier management, quality assurance and export coordination, we work as an extension of your team to help keep each stage connected and accountable.

Our approach combines category knowledge, structured checkpoints and clear communication — so your collection moves from idea to shipment with fewer surprises.

Our promise

The right product. The right supplier. A clear process from sampling through shipment.

Quality governance

Quality built into the process.

Multiple checkpoints help identify issues early and protect product consistency before dispatch.

TIER 01
⌁

Fabric & trim inspection

Fabric grading, GSM verification, shrinkage and colour-fastness checks prior to cutting, based on the agreed test plan.

TIER 02
◉

Pre-production & inline QC

Sewing-line reviews, seam checks, print and embroidery placement verification, with corrective actions tracked.

TIER 03
✓

Final AQL audit

Random sampling, measurement review, packing checks and shipment-readiness reporting to the agreed AQL level.

TESTING
⌗

Lab coordination

Support for relevant testing through accredited laboratories, according to buyer, product and market requirements.
